# Palestine
August 2026. This report breaks down every angle — military, humanitarian, economic, legal, environmental, generational, and geopolitical — so you can understand not just what's happening, but why it matters and where it might be headed.

Every claim in this report is sourced from publicly available, verifiable sources: UN agencies (OCHA, UNRWA, WHO, UNEP, IPC), international courts (ICC, ICJ), peer-reviewed academic journals (The Lancet Global Health), news organizations (Al Jazeera, AP, Reuters, The Guardian, Financial Times, NPR, PBS, The Hill, The New Yorker), human rights organizations (CPJ, Palestinian Journalists Syndicate, Addameer, PHRI, Healthcare Workers Watch, Boycott Bloody Insurance), congressional reports (CRS), corporate filings, and official government statements. Every statistic, every name, every legal citation, and every dollar figure is traceable to a real source. There are no fictional characters, no invented quotes, no fabricated documents, and no hypothetical scenarios presented as fact. The report explicitly distinguishes between confirmed facts, requested warrants, allegations in ongoing trials, and estimates from peer-reviewed studies. Where there is uncertainty or debate (such as the undercounting of deaths), the report presents multiple sources and explains the methodology behind each. The only "stories" are the documented, named victims whose deaths have been reported by multiple independent sources. No made up stories.
